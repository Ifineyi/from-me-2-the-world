01 / about

Who's behind the cube

A computational scientist with one foot in theoretical physics, one in aerospace engineering, and a third (yes, three) in quantitative finance.

I build models — sometimes to understand stars, sometimes to price derivatives, always because the problem is interesting enough to pull apart. Every discipline on the front face of the cube feeds the others: the mathematics is the substrate, the physics is the why, the code is the proof.

This portfolio is organised as a Rubik's cube because a flat resume never told the whole story. Rotate it. Flip a square. Every face is a different lens on the same body of work.
